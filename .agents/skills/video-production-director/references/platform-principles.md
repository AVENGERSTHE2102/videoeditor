# Video principles from platform guidance

Use these as useful signals, not a formula for reach. Recommendations vary by viewer and platform, and change over time.

- YouTube says Shorts discovery is personalized and considers whether people choose to watch, how long they stay, average view duration, viewed percentage, and viewer satisfaction signals. Topic interest, competition, and seasonality also affect reach. Make the audience and promise clear, then earn attention with the video itself.
- YouTube's Shorts analytics advice is to compare both stronger and weaker videos, inspect retention and traffic sources, and learn from patterns without treating one viral result as a guarantee.
- TikTok's creator advice emphasizes an immediate hook, a specific story, bold useful captions, and entertaining, visually clear execution.
- YouTube has stated that original additions such as commentary, distinctive editing, or storytelling matter when using other creators' clips. Prefer original material and preserve permissions/provenance.
